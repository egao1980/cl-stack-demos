== 1. Weak rules in the rule DSL: weights, levels, errors and restarts
==============================================================================
Each form is read by PARSE-RULE. A weak rule has kind :WEAK, no head, and its
cost in RULE-WEIGHT / RULE-LEVEL / RULE-TERMS (also at the front of RULE-PROPERTIES).

  (rule (weak 3 ?x) :- (late ?x))
    kind :weak  weight 3  level 0  terms (?x)
    properties (:weight 3 :level 0 :terms (?x) :source "demo")
  (rule (weak (5 1) ?a ?b) :- (clash ?a ?b))
    kind :weak  weight 5  level 1  terms (?a ?b)
    properties (:weight 5 :level 1 :terms (?a ?b) :source "demo")
  (rule (weak (3 0) ?x) :- (late ?x))
    kind :weak  weight 3  level 0  terms (?x)
    properties (:weight 3 :level 0 :terms (?x) :source "demo")
  (rule (weak ?c ?x) :- (cost ?x ?c))
    kind :weak  weight ?c  level 0  terms (?x)
    properties (:weight ?c :level 0 :terms (?x) :source "demo")
  (rule (weak (-1 2)) :- (bonus ?x) (not (late ?x)))
    kind :weak  weight -1  level 2  terms nil
    properties (:weight -1 :level 2 :terms nil :source "demo")

(weak 3 ?x) and (weak (3 0) ?x) are the same cost: weight 3 at level 0.

Malformed weak rules signal LOGIC-SYNTAX-ERROR (FORM = the whole rule):
  (rule (weak) :- (late ?x))             -> (weak COST TERM...) needs a COST: W or (W L)
  (rule (weak "3" ?x) :- (late ?x))      -> "3" is not a weak COST: W or (W L), W an integer or a variable, L an integer
  (rule (weak 1.5 ?x) :- (late ?x))      -> 1.5 is not a weak COST: W or (W L), W an integer or a variable, L an integer
  (rule (weak (3 ?l) ?x) :- (late ?x))   -> (3 ?l) is not a weak COST: W or (W L), W an integer or a variable, L an integer
  (rule (weak (3 1 2) ?x) :- (late ?x))  -> (3 1 2) is not a weak COST: W or (W L), W an integer or a variable, L an integer
  (rule (weak 3 ?x))                     -> a weak constraint needs at least one body literal
  (rule (flagged ?x) :- (late ?x) (weak 1 ?x)) -> (weak 1 ?x) is not a body literal (atom, built-in or (not …))

Safety: (rule (weak ?w ?x) :- (late ?x)) parses, but RULE-UNSAFE-VARIABLES = (?w):
the weight ?w is a head variable that no positive body literal binds.

== 4. Explanations: why is this schedule penalised?
==============================================================================
The :lex optimum's penalties and rewards, with the ground bodies that fired:
  (-1 0 preferred "build")     <- ((assign "build" 2) (preferred "build" 2))
  (-1 0 preferred "docs")      <- ((assign "docs" 1) (preferred "docs" 1))
  (3 0 late "review")          <- ((assign "review" 4) (late-in "review" 4))
  (3 0 overtime "review")      <- ((assign "review" 4) (slot-cost 4 3))

Why is "review" late? The fixed fact in that grounding is (late-in "review" 4). EXPLAIN:
  (late-in "review" 4)
    by (rule (late-in ?t ?s) :- (option ?t ?s) (due ?t ?d) (> ?s ?d))
    with ?t = "review", ?s = 4, ?d = 2
    built-ins: (> 4 2)
      (option "review" 4)
        by (rule (option ?t ?s) :- (task ?t) (slot ?s) (not (blocked ?t ?s)))
        with ?t = "review", ?s = 4
        not in the model: (blocked "review" 4)
          (task "review")  base fact
          (slot 4)  base fact
      (due "review" 2)  base fact
  height 2; base facts (due "review" 2) (slot 4) (task "review")

Why not on time? "review" is due at 2. Slot 1: EXPLAIN (option "review" 1) is a why-not:
  rule (rule (option ?t ?s) :- (task ?t) (slot ?s) (not (blocked ?t ?s)))
  fails at literal 2: (not (blocked "review" 1))
  and (blocked "review" 1) holds:
    (blocked "review" 1)
      by (rule (blocked ?t ?s) :- (needs ?t ?p) (away ?p ?s))
      with ?t = "review", ?s = 1, ?p = cy
        (needs "review" cy)  base fact
        (away cy 1)  base fact

Slot 2 has room (at most 3 tasks), but "test" is there. What if "review" moves to slot 2 too?
  WEAK-COST ((1 . 5) (0 . -2)) (sum 3, against 4): the new penalty is (5 1 clash "review" "test")
  and its fixed fact (shares "review" "test" cy) holds because
    (shares "review" "test" cy)
      by (rule (shares ?a ?b ?p) :- (needs ?a ?p) (needs ?b ?p) (< ?a ?b))
      with ?a = "review", ?b = "test", ?p = cy
      built-ins: (< "review" "test")
        (needs "review" cy)  base fact
        (needs "test" cy)  base fact
  A level-1 cost outweighs any level-0 saving, so the :lex optimum keeps the
  two apart and pays 3 for lateness plus 3 for overtime.
