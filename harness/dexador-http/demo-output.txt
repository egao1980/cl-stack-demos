== Demo 2: the TOTAL deadline and the read timeout, HTTP and HTTPS (#24)
========================================================================
Each origin stays silent after the request head, then answers.
  http, silent 5 s, :timeout 2.0                       => HTTP-TIMEOUT-ERROR: dexador: total timeout after 2.0 s after 2.01 s
  https, silent 5 s, :timeout 2.0                      => HTTP-TIMEOUT-ERROR: dexador: total timeout after 2.0 s after 2.01 s
  https, silent 6 s, (:connect 5 :read 1)              => HTTP-TIMEOUT-ERROR: dexador: read timeout after 1.0 s after 1.02 s
  http -> 301 -> https, silent 6 s, (:connect 5 :read 1) => HTTP-TIMEOUT-ERROR: dexador: read timeout after 1.0 s after 1.02 s
  https, silent 6 s, (:read 1.5)                       => HTTP-TIMEOUT-ERROR: dexador: read timeout after 1.5 s after 1.52 s
  (0.1.5: both https rows with :read returned the response after 6 s; the TLS reads
   went through cl+ssl's socket BIO, which never sees the fd-stream timeout)

The read timeout bounds silence, not the whole body:
  https, 8 octets 0.5 s apart, (:read 1) => HTTP-RESPONSE 200, body "........", after 4.07 s

A streamed HTTPS body (:want-stream t) keeps the read timeout after send returns:
  send => HTTP-RESPONSE 200 after 0.01 s (headers only)
  first body read, the origin silent for 6 s => HTTP-TIMEOUT-ERROR: dexador: read timeout after 2.0 s on the response body after 2.01 s
