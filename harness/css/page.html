The model is the stylesheet

Every rule on this page was built with make-rule and make-declaration, not written as text. Selectors are parsed into compounds, declaration values are validated and normalised, and the serializer writes either a readable or a minified form.

Validated on construction

A value such as red;}body{x:y is refused with a css-invalid-value condition. A handler can supply a replacement with use-value or drop the item with skip-invalid.

Light and dark from custom properties

Colours live in one theme. The rules only say var(--fg); the theme maps it to a light or a dark value through prefers-color-scheme.

(css:make-css-theme
  :light '(("bg" . "#fdfdfc") ("fg" . "#1f2328") ("accent" . "#0b5cd5"))
  :dark  '(("bg" . "#14161a") ("fg" . "#e4e6e9") ("accent" . "#7ab0ff"))
  :toggle-attribute "data-theme")

How pruning decides

Which rules does used-rules keep?

A selector may match when each of its compounds is satisfied by some single element: its type, its ids and its classes. Combinators, attribute selectors and pseudo-classes count as satisfied, so anything it cannot decide is kept.

  • table th is kept: both elements are on the page.
  • aside and .unused are dropped: nothing here matches.
  • @keyframes and @font-face are always kept.
