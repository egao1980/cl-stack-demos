== Demo 3: run-promise, gather and await (event-protocol 0.3.0, #26)
========================================================================
asyncio.run(asyncio.gather(get(a), get(b), get(c))), each answer 1 s late:
  => (200 200 200) after 1.01 s (one at a time would take 3 s)
  run-promise returned although the origin still has 9 idle keep-alive connections
  open from the clients' pools. await stops the loop once the promise settles;
  event-protocol 0.2.3's run waited 61.9 s for idle connections (http-backend-async#79).
    origin  conn 8    0.83 s  /delay/1?n=c            User-Agent: -
    origin  conn 3    0.83 s  /delay/1?n=b            User-Agent: -
    origin  conn 9    0.83 s  /delay/1?n=a            User-Agent: -

gather keeps argument order, whatever order the answers come in:
  => 0.60 s: conn=8 path=/delay/0.6 | conn=3 path=/delay/0.1 | conn=9 path=/delay/0.3

await on a promise, not only on a (lambda (resolve reject)) starter:
  (run-promise (lambda () (defer-promise *event-loop* (lambda () 42)))) => 42 in 0.000 s
  a thunk that returns a plain value => :NOT-A-PROMISE in 0.000 s

asyncio.wait_for: :timeout signals await-timeout:
  sleep-promise 5 s under :timeout 0.5 => AWAIT-TIMEOUT after 0.50 s, await-timeout-seconds 0.5

A rejected promise is re-signalled by run-promise:
  get-async to a closed port => HTTP-CONNECTION-ERROR after 0.00 s (typep http:http-error: T)
